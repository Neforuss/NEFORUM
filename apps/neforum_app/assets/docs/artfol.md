# Artfol setup guide

This guide adds Artfol to NEFORUM. Each artwork post then goes to the Artfol app on your phone, or opens artfol.app on a computer, with your title, caption and tags ready to copy, and you post it.

## Steps

1. In NEFORUM, open **Accounts** and click **Add account**.
2. Click **Art and portfolios** and choose **Artfol**.
3. Optionally type a label, for example your Artfol username.
4. Click **Add Artfol**.

## Steps: posting artwork

1. In the composer, turn on **Artwork** under **Post to** and select your Artfol account.
2. Add your images, a title, the caption and tags.
3. Set the rating and **Made with AI**.
4. Click the **Post to** button. NEFORUM copies the caption to the clipboard.
5. On a phone, pick **Artfol** in the share sheet. On a computer, NEFORUM opens <https://artfol.app/>; sign in if asked.
6. Start a new post.
7. On a computer, click **Save media to Downloads** in the hand-off window and add the images to the post.
8. Copy the **Title** into the title field.
9. Paste the caption.
10. Copy the **Tags** into the tags field.
11. Choose the categories, and a challenge if you are entering one.
12. Post it.
13. Back in NEFORUM, click **I posted it**.

## How it works

Artfol, an AI-free social portfolio for artists, has no public API. NEFORUM therefore hands your images to the Artfol app, or opens the website, and you post.

- The post's title, text and tags go to the title, caption and tags.
- **Made with AI:** Artfol does not accept AI-generated art. NEFORUM refuses AI-made artwork for Artfol.
- **Rating:** Artfol is for a general audience. NEFORUM shows a warning for Adult artwork.
- **Ask not to train AI:** Artfol is AI-free, so there is nothing to set.
- Artfol only receives artwork. A post without **Artwork** turned on, or without an image, shows an error and is not handed off.
- Artfol's exact web fields could not be checked without signing in. Follow what the page shows.

## Limits

- Artfol publishes no media limits. Older reviews say one image per post; this is not confirmed now.
- NEFORUM sends JPEG or PNG images.
