# Platform icons

Each file is the icon the platform's own website declares (web app manifest,
`apple-touch-icon` or favicon), taken on 2026-10-27 and turned into a
256 × 256 tile: an icon that fills a square keeps its background, a
rounded-square app icon is cropped to fill the tile, any other mark is centred
on white. YouTube Posts uses `youtube.png`.

The icons and the names are trademarks of their owners. NEFORUM shows them
only to identify each platform; it is not affiliated with or endorsed by any
of them.
