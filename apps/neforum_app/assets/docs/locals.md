# Locals setup guide

This guide connects Locals (locals.com) by **hand-off only**: NEFORUM copies your post and opens your community on Locals, and you paste it and click **Publish**. Nothing to set up, no API key, no sign-in.

## Steps

1. In NEFORUM, add an account and choose **Locals**.
2. Optionally type a label, for example your Locals name.
3. Under **Community**, enter the community you post in, as in its address: for `name.locals.com`, enter `name`. You can also paste the address. Leave it empty to open your Locals feed instead.
4. Tap **Add Locals**. That's it.

## Steps: when you publish

1. Publish your post. NEFORUM copies your text to the clipboard and opens your community (or <https://locals.com/feed>). Sign in there once if you are not signed in yet.
2. Click **Write a Post** and press Ctrl+V (⌘V on a Mac) to paste your text.
   - In the Locals app, open **My Communities**, tap **+** and pick the community.
3. To add photos, a video or a sound file, click **Save media to Downloads** in NEFORUM, then pick them under **Add Content** on Locals. Keep the page open until the upload ends.
4. Click **Publish**.

## How it works

### Why there is no automatic posting

- Locals publishes **no API for posting**.
- Locals' composer is only shown to signed-in members, and NEFORUM has not been able to study it yet, so the NEFORUM browser extension does not fill it in. The text goes on the clipboard.

### Who can post

Locals says: "Only supporters can leave posts or comments in communities that have them enabled." The creator and team post in their own community; a member who does not support it cannot post there.

## Limits

From Locals' help centre ("How do I upload videos, podcasts and photos?", updated 2024-04-03).

- Text: Locals publishes no character limit. The Locals app keeps **Publish** greyed out below **10 characters**.
- Up to **10 photos** per post: PNG, JPEG, GIF (keep GIFs to a few frames) and WebP.
- Videos: MPEG, MP4, Ogg, MOV, WebM, WMV, FLV, 3GP and 3G2. Sound: MP3, M4A and WAV.
- Members: videos and sound up to **5 minutes** and under **200 MB**. Creators and their team: by the community's plan. NEFORUM warns above the member limit and lets a creator go ahead.
- Locals does not say whether a post can hold more than one video, or photos with a video, so NEFORUM sends one video or sound file alone.
- Articles (a title of at least 5 characters, an optional subtitle and a JPEG, PNG or GIF cover) are a separate kind of post that you write on Locals. NEFORUM hands off ordinary posts.
- Locals has no threads: a thread is joined into one post.

### Known limitations

- Large uploads need the Locals page kept open until they finish. Locals' **Auto-submit** switch publishes the post when the upload ends.
- The Android app is `com.locals.localsapp` (linked from locals.com), but Locals does not document a share target, so NEFORUM does not share files to it.
