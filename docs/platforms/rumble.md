# Rumble

NEFORUM reaches Rumble by **hand-off**. Nothing to set up, no developer app, no sign-in.

| Tier | What you need | Cost | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Hand-off** (only tier) | Any Rumble account | Free | Rumble's upload page opens with your title, description and tags ready to paste; you pick the video and publish. |

Status as of October 2026.

## Why there is no automatic upload

Rumble has no upload API that a personal account can use. Its developer offerings cover live streams, and upload access for publishers has only been arranged with approved partners on request. Filling in Rumble's upload form automatically would go against its terms, so the last step (pick the file, press publish) is yours.

## Setting it up

1. In NEFORUM, add an account, open **Video sites** and choose **Rumble**.
2. Optionally type a label (for example your channel name). Click **Connect**.

## What happens when you publish

1. NEFORUM opens <https://rumble.com/upload.php> (sign in if asked) and puts your **description** on the clipboard.
2. In the hand-off window, click **Save media to Downloads**, then pick the video in Rumble's file picker (or drag it in).
3. Copy the **Title** and the **Tags** from the hand-off window into Rumble's fields and paste the description.
4. Choose the category, licence and visibility Rumble asks for, and publish.

On a phone NEFORUM offers the video to the Rumble app through the share sheet.

## Tips

- The post's **title** becomes the video title. Without one, NEFORUM uses the first line of the text (up to 100 characters).
- A Rumble post needs a video: NEFORUM shows an error for text-only posts.
- To post the same video to YouTube and Odysee automatically and to Rumble at the same time, add those accounts too: one press publishes there and opens Rumble ready to finish.
