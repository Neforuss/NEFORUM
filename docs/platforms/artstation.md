# ArtStation setup guide

This guide adds ArtStation to NEFORUM. Each artwork post then opens ArtStation's new project page with your title, description and tags ready to copy, and you publish the project.

## Steps

1. In NEFORUM, open **Accounts** and click **Add account**.
2. Click **Art and portfolios** and choose **ArtStation**.
3. Optionally type a label, for example your ArtStation username.
4. Click **Add ArtStation**.

## Steps: posting artwork

1. In the composer, turn on **Artwork** under **Post to** and select your ArtStation account.
2. Add your images or MP4 clips, a title, the description and tags.
3. Set the rating, **Made with AI** and **Ask not to train AI**.
4. Click the **Post to** button. NEFORUM opens <https://www.artstation.com/myartstation/projects/new> and puts the description on the clipboard.
5. Sign in to ArtStation (or with your Epic Games account) if asked.
6. In the hand-off window, click **Save media to Downloads**.
7. Add the images to the project. Add MP4 clips as a **Video Clip**.
8. Copy the **Title** into the title field and paste the description.
9. Crop the cover thumbnail.
10. Under **Categorization**, choose the **Mediums** and the **Subject Matter**.
11. Choose the **Software Used**.
12. Copy the **Tags** into the tags field.
    - With **Ask not to train AI** on, the tags include **NoAI**.
    - With **Made with AI** on, they include **CreatedWithAI**.
13. If the rating is Mature or Adult, tick the **mature content** checkbox.
14. Click **Publish**.
15. Back in NEFORUM, click **I posted it**.

## How it works

ArtStation has no public API, no developer program and no app keys. Its site uses ArtStation or Epic Games sign-in with bot protection, and since September 2026 it blocks AI crawlers and automated scraping tools at the network edge. Only you, in your own signed-in browser, can upload. NEFORUM therefore prepares the project and you press Publish.

- The post's **title** becomes the project title. Without one, NEFORUM uses the first line of the text.
- The post's text is the project description. ArtStation has no co-owner field, so credit collaborators there.
- **Rating:** ArtStation asks you to tick the mature content checkbox for mature work. The cover thumbnail and your avatar must not show mature content.
- **Ask not to train AI:** ArtStation now adds the **NoAI** tag by default. NEFORUM puts it in the tags too, so it stays on.
- **Made with AI:** NEFORUM adds the **CreatedWithAI** tag, which marks AI-made work.
- ArtStation only receives artwork. A post without **Artwork** turned on, or without an image or clip, shows an error and is not handed off.
- Updating a project later does not notify your followers again.
- With the NEFORUM browser extension on a computer, the extension adds the files, title, description and tags where it finds the fields. You do the remaining steps.
- ArtStation's phone apps describe browsing, liking and following, with no upload feature, so on a phone NEFORUM opens the page in your browser.

## Limits

- Images: larger than 400 x 400 and smaller than 10,000 x 10,000 pixels, under 10 MB. Images outside these limits do not upload. NEFORUM shows an error for them.
- ArtStation re-encodes images to JPEG at 90% quality, 1920 pixels wide, and serves 4K where available. It recommends exporting JPEG at 100% quality, 3840 pixels wide, in sRGB.
- Video clips: MP4, up to 1 minute. Free accounts take 25 MB and 2K; Premium takes 250 MB and 4K.
- YouTube, Vimeo, Marmoset Viewer and Sketchfab embeds are added on ArtStation itself.
- ArtStation sets no limit on images per project. NEFORUM hands over up to 100.
- Hiding a project from both ArtStation.com and your website needs Premium.
