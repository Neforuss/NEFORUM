# Text cards: your @handle and the look of the words

Some networks need an image in every post: Instagram, Pinterest, TikTok photo posts and Snapchat. When a post is only words, NEFORUM makes a text card for them: a picture of your words set large, with the NEFORUM signature and your handle.

- With **Use the text card on every network**, every network that takes images gets the same card, each at its own shape.
- **YouTube Posts** takes the words alone or with an image. It gets the card whenever another network in the post does, and Customize can make it **Text only** or always **With a text card** for that post.
- **YouTube videos** uploads videos only, so it never gets a card.

The text card editor sets your own @handle on the cards and how the words look, once for every network.

## Steps

1. Write the post and pick where it goes. Each network that gets a card shows it in its preview.
2. When two or more networks get a card, **Text card for every network** sits above the previews. It is the one place that changes every network's card. On a phone it starts folded: tap the arrow beside its title to open it. When only one network gets a card, tap **Text & @handle…** under that card instead; it opens the same controls.
3. Type the handle you want in **Handle on every card**, for example `@neforum.app`. While the field is empty it shows each network's own handle. What you type replaces that handle on every card, and the line under the field names the handles it replaces. To go back to each network's own handle, tap the reset button in the field or clear it.
4. Tap **Style the words and the handle** to unfold the rest of the controls. The button says what is already set, and the section starts unfolded on a card you restyled. Under **Handle**, set its **Size**, its **Place** (Automatic, Bottom left, Bottom centre, Bottom right, Top left, Top centre, Top right) and its **Colour**.
5. Under **Words**, set the **Size** of the words (with the slider, or a step at a time with the smaller and larger buttons beside it), the **Line spacing**, the **Position** (Top, Middle or Bottom), the **Colours** of the words and of the card, and the **Margins**.
6. Under **Card**, pick the **Shape**: **Network's own** (4:5 on Instagram, 9:16 on TikTok, square elsewhere), **Square 1:1**, **Landscape 16:9** or **Landscape 1.91:1**. Pick the **Layout**: **Full card**, or **Quote card** for your words on a card with rounded corners. Type an **Attribution under the words** if you want one, such as `- Ada Lovelace`. To make some words bold, put two asterisks around them in your post, like `**this**`; one asterisk on each side makes them italic.
7. Every change shows at once on the master card and on every network's preview. **Reset handle and words** puts the card back, but keeps its shape and layout.
8. Save, schedule or publish. When the post is saved, NEFORUM makes every network's card with these choices.

## How it works

### Shape and layout

The shape is the same on every network that shows the picture whole. Instagram's feed, Facebook and LinkedIn show pictures from 4:5 up to 1.91:1, so both landscape shapes go to them as they are.

TikTok and stories show pictures full screen. They get a 9:16 picture with your card whole inside it, clear of their buttons, and the note under that network's preview says so. When **Fill the whole frame** is off for TikTok in Settings > Posting, TikTok gets the landscape card as it is. Snapchat always gets the 9:16 frame. A network that takes neither landscape shape gets room added around the card; NEFORUM never cuts a card to fit.

Settings > Text cards sets the shape and layout that new posts start with, and changing them in the editor changes them there too. A card design draws at each network's own shape.

A quote card sets your words in regular weight on a card with rounded corners. The room around that card is a slightly darker shade of the card's colour (lighter on a dark card), or your background photo when there is one. The card keeps its colour over the photo.

### Bold, italic and the attribution

Words between two asterisks, `**like this**`, are bold on the card. Words between single asterisks, `*like this*`, are italic, and words between three are both. The asterisks never show on the card. Asterisks with spaces around them (`5 * 3`) or inside a word stay as they are, and bold or italic never runs past a blank line.

Networks that read Markdown (Reddit, Lemmy, Discord, Telegram and the blogs) get your words as written. The others get them without those asterisks, so a caption never shows them. A blank line between paragraphs leaves a line of space on the card.

The attribution goes under the words in bold, at their size and lined up with them. The words make room for it, so it is never cut off. The attribution is part of the card's description too.

### Your @handle

The handle you type goes on the card of every network that shows a handle. A network whose **Show @handle** is off keeps it off, and the editor says which ones. NEFORUM keeps up to 80 characters and trims spaces at either end. The handle can be a handle, a name or a short line such as a website.

**Automatic** puts the handle where it always was: at the bottom right beside the NEFORUM signature, or at the bottom left on a plain card. At the top, the handle sits above the words, inside the margins. In the middle of the bottom edge, it keeps clear of the signature.

### Size of the words

A card sets the words as large as they fit, and **Size** works from there. Below 100%, the words get smaller by that much. Above 100%, they grow as far as they still fit, so short words get bigger and long ones are never cut off. When a larger size changes nothing because the words already fill the card, the editor says so under the slider ("Already as large as these words fit on Instagram"), or says how large they did get. The handle's **Size** goes from 60% to 250% of its usual size.

### Colours

Each colour button shows its colour's hex code and opens the colour picker. The picker has swatches, the colour wheel with a brightness slider, and a hex code such as `#FFD60A`. **The theme's** in the picker puts the theme's colour back. Picking **White on black** or **Black on white** under the card also puts the theme's colours back for the words and the card. The handle is the colour of the words, a little faded, until you pick one for it.

### Over a background photo

A card can have a background photo, set under **Background: photo**. The photo has its own **Words** controls, and the words' **Position** there is the same setting as in the editor: what you set in either place shows in both.

For the words' colour, both places show what the card uses, and the card follows the last change you made.

- **Automatic** (white or black, whichever reads on that photo), **White** and **Black** in the photo's controls belong to the photo. They go when the photo goes, so a colour picked for a light photo never stays on the plain card.
- **Other…** in the photo's controls, and the words colour in the editor, apply to every card. If one of those, or a handle colour, would be hard to read on the plain card once the photo is gone (or after you pick another theme), the theme's colour comes back.

When a photo shown whole leaves room on the card, the card's colour fills it.

### Emoji on a card

An emoji on a card, on a caption or on the picture of a sound file is drawn in your device's own emoji font, in colour, on every card style, whatever font the words use. So what a card shows depends on the device that makes it: a device with an older emoji font cannot draw the newest emoji.

- An emoji your device cannot draw is left off the picture, rather than drawn as a blank box or a "no glyph" tile, and a message says which one. The text of the post keeps it, and it shows on the network for anyone whose device has it.
- An emoji your device draws in pieces (Windows 10 draws a flag as two letters) stays on the picture as it draws here, and a message says so.

### Margins and place

**Margins** is the room between the card's edges and everything on it, in pixels on a 1080 pixel wide card (92 is the usual). The footer with the signature moves in with the margins. **Position** puts the words at the top, the middle or the bottom of the room the card leaves them. On a full-screen card for TikTok, Snapchat or a story, the words and the handle still keep clear of the network's own buttons.

### On a card design

A card design is a ready-made look for the card, such as Vinyl, Neon or Polaroid, picked with **Design card…** or set as the default design in Settings. When a design draws the cards, the same controls change that design:

- the size, colour, line spacing and place of its words
- its handle's size, place and colour
- the card's colour (not a photo; a gradient becomes that one colour)
- its margins (24 pixels at least, with a network's safe area on top).

**Design card…** shows the same design afterwards. The design's own text (its signature, its handle, its other lines) always stays where the design puts it.

Once you pick Top, Middle or Bottom, the words go in a band of the card that none of the design's pictures and none of its own text crosses. The pictures are the record, the waveform, and a framed photo once there is a photo. Top uses the topmost such band, Bottom the lowest, and Middle the tallest, centred in it. Where a card leaves only one such band (under the record on Vinyl's square cards), Top, Middle and Bottom all go there, at its top, middle or bottom.

The words shrink to fit that band, down to 28 pixels on a 1080 pixel wide card. At that size a post of a few sentences fits whole under Polaroid's framed photo on a square card. A post too long for the band at that size is cut with an ellipsis. If the margins and the handle's size leave no room at all for the words, or for a handle the editor placed, they are left off the card rather than printed over a picture.

Either way, a warning says so for the network where it happens. It shows under that network's card and in the editor, whether the editor is folded or not, and says what helps: another position, when NEFORUM found one that shows every word, or else shorter words, smaller margins or a smaller handle.

A design without a handle of its own (Gradient wave, Vinyl, Neon, Sunset) gets one as soon as you type a handle, in the place the design leaves free:

- Vinyl: top right, beside the record
- Gradient wave: bottom left, above the waveform
- Neon: above the centred signature
- Sunset: top left.

A handle moved with **Place** goes the same way, on its edge, clear of the pictures and of the design's own text, and the words keep clear of it. A design that hides its handle (in **Design card…**) shows none, and the editor says so.

NEFORUM works all of this out each time it draws a card. So it holds on square, portrait and full-screen cards, in whatever order you change things, and when you add a photo later. To turn it off, or on again, use **Keep the words and handle clear of the pictures** under Layout in **Design card…**. With it off, the words and the handle go back to where their place and offsets put them.

A design's words start at their size and shrink to fit. When they come out smaller, the editor says how large they did get.

**Reset handle and words** puts the design back as it was before the editor first changed it. This works until the post is saved, even after the editor is folded or the app is restarted. A post that followed the default design in Settings follows it again. The classic card's own styling is kept for when you turn the design off.

### What stays per network

The handle and the look belong to the post and are the same on every card. Each network keeps its own shape (unless the card has one; see **Shape and layout**), its signature on or off, **Show @handle** on or off, and **Post text too**. The master editor lists what differs between the networks, and **Make them match** sets those the same.

### Saved with the post

The choices are saved with the post, written into each card when it is made, and kept with the post's networks. So a scheduled post, a post you edit again and a post synced to another device all make the same cards. NEFORUM makes a card again only when something it shows changes.

### Cards from before

A card nobody restyles stays exactly as NEFORUM 1.3.4 drew it, and cards already saved are kept as they are.
