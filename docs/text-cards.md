# Text cards: your @handle and the look of the words

When a post is only words, the networks that need an image (Instagram, Pinterest, TikTok photo posts, Snapchat) get a NEFORUM text card: your words set large on a card, with the NEFORUM signature and your handle. With **Use the text card on every network**, every network that takes images gets the same card, each at its own shape. The text card editor sets your own @handle on the cards and how the words look, once for every network.

## Steps

1. Write the post and pick where it goes. Each network that gets a card shows it in its preview.
2. When two or more networks get a card, **Text card for every network** sits above the previews. On a phone it starts folded: tap the arrow beside its title to open it. When only one network gets a card, tap **Text & @handle…** under that card instead; it opens the same controls.
3. Type the handle you want in **Handle on every card**, for example `@neforum.app`. The empty field shows each network's own handle; what you type replaces it on every card. Tap the reset button in the field, or clear it, to go back to each network's own handle.
4. Tap **Style the words and the handle** to unfold the rest (it says what is already set, and starts unfolded on a card you restyled). Under **Handle**, set its **Size**, its **Corner** (Automatic, Bottom left, Bottom right, Top left, Top right) and its **Colour**.
5. Under **Words**, set the **Size** of the words, the **Line spacing**, the **Position** (Top, Middle or Bottom), the **Colours** of the words and of the card, and the **Margins**.
6. Every change shows at once on the master card and on every network's preview. **Reset handle and words** puts the classic card back.
7. Save, schedule or publish. Every network's card is made with these choices when the post is saved.

## How it works

### Your @handle

The handle you type goes on the card of every network that shows a handle. A network whose **Show @handle** is off keeps it off: the editor says which ones. Up to 80 characters are kept, trimmed of spaces at either end; it can be a handle, a name or a short line such as a website. Cards drawn from a design (**Design card…**) show the handle you type too; the design sets its size, place and colour.

**Automatic** puts the handle where it always was: at the bottom right beside the NEFORUM signature, at the bottom left on a plain card. At the top, it sits above the words, inside the margins.

### Size of the words

A card sets the words as large as they fit. **Size** works from there: below 100% the words get smaller by that much, above 100% they grow as far as they still fit, so short words get bigger and long ones are never cut off. The handle's **Size** goes from 60% to 250% of its usual size.

### Colours

Each colour button opens the colour picker: swatches, the colour wheel with a brightness slider, and a hex code such as `#FFD60A`. **The theme's** in the picker puts the theme's colour back. Picking **White on black** or **Black on white** under the card also puts the theme's colours back for the words and the card. Over a background photo, the card's colour fills the room a whole photo leaves, and the words' colour replaces the photo's text colour. The handle is the colour of the words, a little faded, until you pick one for it.

### Margins and place

**Margins** is the room between the card's edges and everything on it, in pixels on a 1080 pixel wide card (92 is the usual). The footer with the signature moves in with it. **Position** puts the words at the top, the middle or the bottom of the room the card leaves them. On a full-screen card for TikTok, Snapchat or a story, the words and the handle still keep clear of the network's own buttons.

### What stays per network

The handle and the look are the post's, the same on every card. Each network keeps its own shape, its signature on or off, **Show @handle** on or off and **Post text too**. The master editor lists what differs between the networks and **Make them match** sets those the same.

### Saved with the post

The choices are saved with the post, written into each card when it is made and kept with the post's networks, so a scheduled post, a post edited again and a post synced to another device all make the same cards. A card is made again only when something it shows changes.

### Cards from before

A card nobody restyles stays exactly as NEFORUM 1.3.4 drew it, and cards already saved are kept as they are.
