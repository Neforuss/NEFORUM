# Text cards: your @handle and the look of the words

When a post is only words, the networks that need an image (Instagram, Pinterest, TikTok photo posts, Snapchat) get a NEFORUM text card: your words set large on a card, with the NEFORUM signature and your handle. With **Use the text card on every network**, every network that takes images gets the same card, each at its own shape. The text card editor sets your own @handle on the cards and how the words look, once for every network.

## Steps

1. Write the post and pick where it goes. Each network that gets a card shows it in its preview.
2. When two or more networks get a card, **Text card for every network** sits above the previews: it is the one place that changes every network's card. On a phone it starts folded: tap the arrow beside its title to open it. When only one network gets a card, tap **Text & @handle…** under that card instead; it opens the same controls.
3. Type the handle you want in **Handle on every card**, for example `@neforum.app`. The empty field shows each network's own handle; what you type replaces it on every card, and the line under the field names the handles it replaces. Tap the reset button in the field, or clear it, to go back to each network's own handle.
4. Tap **Style the words and the handle** to unfold the rest (it says what is already set, and starts unfolded on a card you restyled). Under **Handle**, set its **Size**, its **Place** (Automatic, Bottom left, Bottom centre, Bottom right, Top left, Top centre, Top right) and its **Colour**.
5. Under **Words**, set the **Size** of the words (with the slider, or a step at a time with the smaller and larger buttons beside it), the **Line spacing**, the **Position** (Top, Middle or Bottom), the **Colours** of the words and of the card, and the **Margins**.
6. Every change shows at once on the master card and on every network's preview. **Reset handle and words** puts the card back.
7. Save, schedule or publish. Every network's card is made with these choices when the post is saved.

## How it works

### Your @handle

The handle you type goes on the card of every network that shows a handle. A network whose **Show @handle** is off keeps it off: the editor says which ones. Up to 80 characters are kept, trimmed of spaces at either end; it can be a handle, a name or a short line such as a website.

**Automatic** puts the handle where it always was: at the bottom right beside the NEFORUM signature, at the bottom left on a plain card. At the top, it sits above the words, inside the margins; in the middle of the bottom edge it keeps clear of the signature.

### Size of the words

A card sets the words as large as they fit. **Size** works from there: below 100% the words get smaller by that much, above 100% they grow as far as they still fit, so short words get bigger and long ones are never cut off. When a larger size changes nothing, because the words already fill the card, the editor says so under the slider ("Already as large as these words fit on Instagram"), or says how large they did get. The handle's **Size** goes from 60% to 250% of its usual size.

### Colours

Each colour button shows its colour's hex code and opens the colour picker: swatches, the colour wheel with a brightness slider, and a hex code such as `#FFD60A`. **The theme's** in the picker puts the theme's colour back. Picking **White on black** or **Black on white** under the card also puts the theme's colours back for the words and the card. The handle is the colour of the words, a little faded, until you pick one for it.

### Over a background photo

The words' **Position** and colour are one setting with the **Words** controls of the card's background photo (**Background: photo**): what you set in either shows in both, and the card follows the last change. Over a photo, **Automatic** sets the words in white or black, whichever reads on that photo; **White**, **Black** or any other colour sets them in that colour. The card's colour fills the room a whole photo leaves.

### Margins and place

**Margins** is the room between the card's edges and everything on it, in pixels on a 1080 pixel wide card (92 is the usual). The footer with the signature moves in with it. **Position** puts the words at the top, the middle or the bottom of the room the card leaves them. On a full-screen card for TikTok, Snapchat or a story, the words and the handle still keep clear of the network's own buttons.

### On a card design

When a design draws the cards (one picked with **Design card…**, or the default design in Settings), the same controls change that design: the size, colour, line spacing and place of its words, its handle's size, place and colour, the card's colour (not a photo; a gradient becomes that one colour) and its margins. **Design card…** shows the same design afterwards. Words moved to the top or bottom join the handle already on that edge instead of covering it. A design's words start at their size and shrink to fit; when they come out smaller, the editor says how large they did get. **Reset handle and words** puts the design back as it was when you started changing it here. The classic card's own styling is kept for when you turn the design off.

### What stays per network

The handle and the look are the post's, the same on every card. Each network keeps its own shape, its signature on or off, **Show @handle** on or off and **Post text too**. The master editor lists what differs between the networks and **Make them match** sets those the same.

### Saved with the post

The choices are saved with the post, written into each card when it is made and kept with the post's networks, so a scheduled post, a post edited again and a post synced to another device all make the same cards. A card is made again only when something it shows changes.

### Cards from before

A card nobody restyles stays exactly as NEFORUM 1.3.4 drew it, and cards already saved are kept as they are.
