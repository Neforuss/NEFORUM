Pair with NEFORUM

  1. In NEFORUM open Settings > Browser extension.
  2. Click Pair a browser and type the 6-digit code here.

When you click Open in a NEFORUM hand-off, this browser fills in the post on the platform's page. You check it and press Post.

For sites the extension does not open by itself (for example your Mastodon server), or to fill in again.

Allow the extension on the platforms' sites so it can fill in posts there.
