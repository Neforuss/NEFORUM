# Cara setup guide

This guide adds Cara to NEFORUM. Each artwork post then goes to the Cara app on your phone, or opens cara.app on a computer, with your text ready to paste, and you post it.

## Steps

1. In NEFORUM, open **Accounts** and click **Add account**.
2. Click **Art and portfolios** and choose **Cara**.
3. Optionally type a label, for example your Cara username.
4. Click **Add Cara**.

## Steps: posting artwork

1. In the composer, turn on **Artwork** under **Post to** and select your Cara account.
2. Add your images and the post text (up to 1,200 characters). Tags go into the text as hashtags.
3. Set the rating and **Made with AI**.
4. Click the **Post to** button. NEFORUM copies the text to the clipboard.
5. On a phone, pick **Cara** in the share sheet. On a computer, NEFORUM opens <https://cara.app/>; sign in if asked.
6. Click **Post** to open the composer.
7. On a computer, click **Save media to Downloads** in the hand-off window and add the images to the composer.
8. Paste the post text.
9. Crop the thumbnail.
10. Tick **Add to Portfolio** if the work should show in your portfolio too.
    - Leave it off for AI-generated work and use Cara's AI label.
11. Click **Post**.
12. Back in NEFORUM, click **I posted it**.

## How it works

Cara has no API: no developer program, no OAuth, no keys and no app passwords. Its site refuses programs that are not a browser, and it has fought large scrapes of its artists' work. NEFORUM never automates it: you post from the Cara app or from cara.app.

- Cara has no title field. The post's text is the post, with the tags added as hashtags.
- **Rating:** Cara does not host NSFW work. NEFORUM refuses Adult artwork for Cara and shows a warning for Mature artwork.
- **Made with AI:** Cara's terms keep AI-generated work out of portfolios and ask for an AI label elsewhere. NEFORUM shows a warning and tells you to leave **Add to Portfolio** off.
- **Ask not to train AI:** Cara adds NoAI tags by default and offers Glaze, so there is nothing to set.
- Cara only receives artwork. A post without **Artwork** turned on, or without an image, shows an error and is not handed off.
- Cara cannot change the images of a post afterwards, only its text.
- Albums can only be edited in a desktop browser.

## Limits

- Post text: up to 1,200 characters.
- Images and GIFs (animated GIF covers are in beta). Videos only as YouTube or Sketchfab embeds, added on Cara itself.
- Cara publishes no file size or image limit. One app review reports 10 images per post (unverified), so NEFORUM hands over up to 10.
