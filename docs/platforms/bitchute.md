# BitChute

NEFORUM reaches BitChute by **hand-off**. Nothing to set up, no developer app, no sign-in.

| Tier | What you need | Cost | Result |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Hand-off** (only tier) | A BitChute account with a channel | Free | bitchute.com opens with your title, description and up to three search hashtags ready to paste; you pick the video and a thumbnail and publish. |

Status as of October 2026.

## Why there is no automatic upload

BitChute uploads only through its website: it has no upload API. NEFORUM prepares everything and you finish on bitchute.com.

**Shortcut:** BitChute can import your new YouTube videos by itself. Add your YouTube channel ID in BitChute's settings and ask BitChute support to verify it ("Automatically send your YouTube videos to BitChute" in BitChute's help). Then publishing to YouTube with NEFORUM also brings the video to BitChute.

## Setting it up

1. In NEFORUM, add an account, open **Video sites** and choose **BitChute**.
2. Optionally type a label. Click **Connect**.

## What happens when you publish

1. NEFORUM opens <https://www.bitchute.com/> (sign in if asked) and puts your **description** on the clipboard.
2. Click BitChute's **upload** icon (top right).
3. In the hand-off window, click **Save media to Downloads**, then pick the video in BitChute's form.
4. Copy the **Title** and the **Tags** (up to three search hashtags) from the hand-off window, paste the description, choose a thumbnail (a frame of the video or your own picture) and the content sensitivity, and publish.

## Limits (BitChute help centre)

- Title up to **100** characters; description up to **3,000** (5,000 for members); up to **three** search hashtags. NEFORUM uses the first three of your tags.
- Video formats: MOV, MP4, AVI, WMV, 3GP, OGG, WEBM, MKV, M4V. 16:9 is preferred.
- A thumbnail is required.
