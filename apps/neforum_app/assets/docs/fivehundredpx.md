# 500px setup guide

This guide adds 500px to NEFORUM. Each artwork post then goes to the 500px app on your phone, or opens 500px.com on a computer, with your title, description and keywords ready to copy, and you publish it.

## Steps

1. In NEFORUM, open **Accounts** and click **Add account**.
2. Click **Art and portfolios** and choose **500px**.
3. Optionally type a label, for example your 500px username.
4. Click **Add 500px**.

## Steps: posting artwork

1. In the composer, turn on **Artwork** under **Post to** and select your 500px account.
2. Add up to 30 photos, a title, the description and keywords as tags.
3. Set the rating.
4. Click the **Post to** button. NEFORUM copies the description to the clipboard.
5. On a phone, pick **500px** in the share sheet. On a computer, NEFORUM opens <https://500px.com/>; sign in if asked.
6. Click **Upload** at the top right.
7. On a computer, click **Save media to Downloads** in the hand-off window and add the photos to the uploader.
8. Copy the **Title** into the title field and paste the description.
9. Copy the **Tags** into the keywords field.
10. Choose a category, and the location, licence and privacy you want.
11. If the rating is Mature or Adult, turn on **NSFW**.
12. Click **Publish**.
13. Back in NEFORUM, click **I posted it**.

## How it works

500px ended free API access on January 24, 2018, and existing keys stopped working on June 15, 2018. Its Lightroom publish plugin is discontinued too. NEFORUM therefore hands your photos to the 500px app, or opens the website, and you publish.

- The post's title, text and tags go to each photo's title, description and keywords.
- **Rating:** 500px has an NSFW switch per photo. NEFORUM reminds you to turn it on for Mature and Adult work.
- **Ask not to train AI:** 500px has no such option, so this changes nothing here.
- 500px only receives artwork. A post without **Artwork** turned on, or without a photo, shows an error and is not handed off.
- 500px adds a watermark to uploads.

## Limits

- Up to 30 photos per upload.
- Free (Basic) accounts get 60 uploads a month. Deleting a photo does not give an upload back.
- 500px recommends JPEG. NEFORUM sends JPEG.
